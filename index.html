01 / ABOUT

Finance taught me the numbers.
Operations taught me what breaks behind them.

I started my career in traditional finance — reconciliations, reporting, compliance, month-end closes, and a lot of spreadsheets.

Over time, I became more interested in what was happening behind the numbers: why reconciliations broke, why collections became reactive, where revenue quietly leaked, and why processes that worked at a smaller scale started to strain as businesses grew.

That curiosity pulled me closer to Revenue Operations — AR and O2C, process improvement, automation, and building financial systems that hold up at scale.
